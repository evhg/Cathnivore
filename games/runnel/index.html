    How to play

    Hi, I'm Cath. These are my fields, and they're thirsty. Here's how you help.

    1. Tap a tile to turn it clockwise. Right-click or Shift+click turns it back.
    2. Bring water from the spring in the middle to every tile. Fields sprout when it reaches them.
    3. No channel may spill: every opening must meet another channel.
    4. Hold a tile to pin it once you're sure of it.

    A new Daily puzzle arrives at midnight UTC. Par is the number of taps the puzzle maker needed.
