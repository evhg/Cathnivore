The Republic of Marrow needs you. Two ultra-processed conglomerates are buying up its farmland, and it's up to a handful of small farmers — led by a cattle rancher the tabloids call "the Cathnivore" — to push them out, one region at a time.

Cathnivore is a free, cooperative engine-building strategy game for one or two players. Open market Stalls, clear the enemy's Outlets and Buyouts, rebut their doubt campaigns, and out-produce two rival companies before Public Trust collapses or the land runs out.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- A six-chapter story campaign that teaches the game as you play, following Cath and four playable allies through a satirical fight against corporate spin.
- Quick Game mode for a full 45-60 minute session any time.
- Solo mode with an AI teammate, or Hot-seat for two players on one device.
- A visible enemy plan every round — you always know where the next threat is landing, which is the heart of every decision.
- Dozens of Improvements and Schemes to build your farm's engine and outmaneuver the opposition.
- Everything drawn in clean, hand-made-looking vector art. No stock art, no generated images.

A WORK OF SATIRE
Every place, company and person in Cathnivore is fictional. It pokes fun at marketing spin, sponsored research and corporate buyouts in general — never at real companies, real people, or real farmers. Nothing in the game is medical or health advice.

NO TRACKING, EVER
Cathnivore collects no data, shows no ads, and has no accounts or in-app purchases. Every save stays on your device. The game works fully offline once installed.
