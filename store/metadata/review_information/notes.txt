Cathnivore is a free, single-player-or-two-player cooperative strategy game. There is no login, no
account, and no server: all game state lives on the device via localStorage-equivalent storage.

There is nothing to sign in with, so no demo account is needed.

To review the tutorial: from the title screen tap "Campaign", then "Fresh Meat" (chapter 1). The first
few steps are guided — only the action being taught is tappable at each step — and finish in under 10
minutes.

Every place, company and person depicted (Hollowell Group, Candor Health, the Republic of Marrow, and
every named character) is fictional. The game satirizes corporate marketing and lobbying tactics in
general; it does not depict, name, or allude to any real company, product, or person, and makes no
health or medical claims.

The app collects no data and shows no ads or in-app purchases (App Privacy: Data Not Collected).
