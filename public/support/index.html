Support

Cathnivore is a free, cooperative engine-building strategy game. You and (optionally) an AI or second human teammate play local producers pushing back against two much larger conglomerates, Hollowell and Candor, region by region.

How to play

Open the game and choose Campaign for a guided six-chapter story that introduces the rules a few at a time, or Quick Game to jump straight into a full match. The in-game How to Play reference (from the title screen) covers every action, Improvement, Scheme, Agenda card and rule in full, and can be reopened at any time.
